PROJECT PDFS — HOW TO UPDATE THEM

Your 10 supplied project PDFs are already placed in assets/documents.

To replace or update any project later:
1. Open the website folder in VS Code.
2. Open assets > documents.
3. Replace the relevant PDF with your new PDF.
4. Keep the exact existing filename (listed below).
5. Refresh the project page.

The project pages are normal editable HTML/CSS/JavaScript files.
The PDFs are separate files, so you can replace them without editing the page code.

Note: The website uses PDF.js from a CDN to display pages inline. An internet connection is required.
Links embedded in PDFs (such as prototype videos) are restored as clickable areas on the rendered pages.
