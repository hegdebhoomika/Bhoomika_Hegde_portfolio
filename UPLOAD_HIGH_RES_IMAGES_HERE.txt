YOUR ONLY TASK:
Replace the placeholder image files with your own high-resolution JPG images.
Do not edit HTML or CSS.

HOW TO REPLACE:
1. Extract the website ZIP.
2. Open the folder: assets > uploads > [project name].
3. You will see numbered files such as 01.jpg, 02.jpg, 03.jpg.
4. Replace each placeholder with your own exported slide/image, using the exact same filename.
5. Keep the file format as JPG. If your image is PNG, export/save a JPG copy.
6. Refresh the corresponding project page.

IMAGE ORDER:
Upload your images in the order you want them to appear on the page.
For multi-page documentation, export each slide/page as a separate high-resolution image.
